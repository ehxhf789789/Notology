# <img src="images/icons/calendar.png" width="24" height="24"> Calendar & Memos

Manage your memos and to-dos by date. Comments written in your notes are automatically linked to the calendar, so you can view your schedule and notes in one place.

---

## Opening the Calendar

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Shortcut** | `Ctrl + Shift + C` |
| **Panel** | Press `Ctrl + →` to open the right panel, which includes the calendar |
| **Collapsed bar** | When the panel is collapsed, click the calendar icon button |

> 📸 **GIF placeholder** — `images/calendar-open.gif`
>
> **Shot**: Opening the calendar with the Ctrl+Shift+C shortcut
> **Steps**: ① Press Ctrl+Shift+C on the keyboard → ② The right panel slides open → ③ The calendar is displayed
> **Screen area**: Full screen (editor + right panel opening animation)
> **Highlight**: The panel sliding open after the shortcut is pressed
> **Duration**: 3~5s

---

## Monthly Calendar

The calendar shows one month at a time, with navigation controls at the top.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **◀ / ▶ buttons** | Move to the previous or next month |
| **Today highlight** | Today's date is visually highlighted |
| **Memo dot** | A small dot appears on dates that have memos |

> 📸 **GIF placeholder** — `images/calendar-navigate.gif`
>
> **Shot**: Clicking the month navigation buttons, viewing today's highlight, and checking dots on dates with memos
> **Steps**: ① Click the ◀ button to go to the previous month → ② Click the ▶ button to go to the next month → ③ Observe today's date highlighted → ④ Hover over a date that has a memo dot
> **Screen area**: Calendar area within the right panel only
> **Highlight**: Today's date highlight style and the small dots on dates with memos
> **Duration**: 5~8s

## Selecting a Date

Click a date on the calendar to display its memos and to-do list below.

> **Shot**: Clicking a date on the calendar to display its memo list

## Memo / To-Do Tabs

Items for the selected date are divided into two tabs.

| Tab | Content | Display |
|-----|---------|---------|
| **📝 Memos** | Comments written for that date | Text message list |
| **✅ To-dos** | Checklist items for that date | Checkbox list |

Each tab shows a **badge with the count** of today's items, so you can quickly see how many tasks you have for the day.

> 📸 **GIF placeholder** — `images/calendar-tabs.gif`
>
> **Shot**: Switching between the Memo tab and To-do tab and checking badge numbers
> **Steps**: ① Click the 📝 Memo tab → memo list is displayed → ② Click the ✅ To-do tab → checkbox list is displayed → ③ Check the badge numbers next to each tab
> **Screen area**: Bottom of the right panel (tab bar + list area)
> **Highlight**: Content changing when switching tabs, and the badge numbers next to each tab
> **Duration**: 5~8s

| Action | Description |

> **Screen area**: Full screen (memo click in the right panel + hover window appearing in the center)
> **Highlight**: The moment the hover window pops up after clicking the memo
> **Duration**: 3~5s

> 📸 **GIF placeholder** — `images/calendar-check-memo.gif`

## Calendar Badge on Collapsed Bar

When the right panel is collapsed, the calendar button shows a **badge with today's memo/to-do count**. This lets you quickly check your schedule without opening the panel.

> 📸 **GIF placeholder** — `images/calendar-badge.gif`
>
> **Shot**: Showing the badge number on the calendar button when the right panel is collapsed
> **Steps**: ① Show the right panel in its collapsed state → ② Focus on the badge number next to the calendar icon → ③ Click the badge to open the panel
> **Screen area**: Right edge of the screen (collapsed bar area only)
> **Highlight**: The badge number displayed next to the calendar icon
> **Duration**: 3~5s

## Usage Examples

Here are some ways to make the most of the calendar.

| Use Case | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Daily log** | Leave comments about your daily work or thoughts, and review them by date in the calendar |
| **Project deadlines** | Add deadline comments to project notes to track schedules in the calendar |
| **Meeting schedule** | Add date comments to meeting notes to see all meetings at a glance in the calendar |
| **Daily review** | At the end of the day, review today's memos in the calendar to reflect and organize |
| **Task management** | Use checklists to manage what needs to be done today |

> **💡 Tip**: When you add a comment to a note and specify a date, it automatically links to the calendar. See the [Editor Advanced](EN-Editor-Advanced) page for more details.
